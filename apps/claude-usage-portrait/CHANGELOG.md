# Claude Usage Portrait

## 0.3.0

A calmer usage screen. Your weekly limit is one big number, the account email is gone in favour of a plan badge, the status line is one dot and one word, the four tabs are page dots you turn with the dial, and old readings dim instead of shouting. The player bar is bigger and only shows while something is playing, and pressing the dial plays or pauses.

## 0.2.0

A mini player along the bottom (art, title, previous, play and pause, next). The front button opens an app picker with home first, so you can switch apps without five Mode presses.

## 0.1.1

The settings page now has the dial end and invert options.

## 0.1.0

A portrait fork of Joey Eamigh's Claude Usage (github.com/JoeyEamigh/joey-bridgething-apps). Every screen is redrawn for a Car Thing standing on its side, with a rotation setting for which end the dial is on and an option to invert the dial.
